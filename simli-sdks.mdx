## Overview

The Simli SDKs let you create interactive AI avatars with full control over the tech stack. They're ideal for apps with custom voice agents, or for integrating with existing systems. You send the audio the avatar should speak, and get back its lip-synced video and voice.

| SDK | Runs | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [JavaScript SDK](/api-reference/javascript) (`simli-client`) | In the browser | A Simli API key, and a backend route that creates session tokens, so the key never goes in the browser. |
| [Python SDK](/api-reference/python) (`simli-ai`) | In your Python program | A Simli API key. |

Each SDK has three pages:

- **Guide**: install, connect, send audio, stop, and errors and retries.
- **Trinity-2**: the video size, the avatar's position, the camera, backgrounds and foregrounds.
- **API reference**: every class, method, event and type.

Building a voice agent? [LiveKit Agents](/api-reference/livekit) and [Pipecat](/api-reference/pipecat) handle the audio and video for you. See [all starters](/api-reference/samples/all-samples) for complete example apps, or join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/yQx49zNF4d) for support and updates.
