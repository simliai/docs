<Note>
Coming soon to Simli Compose.
</Note>

A Trinity-2 avatar will be able to play an animation on command during a Simli Compose session, such as a wave or a gesture while it talks. Faces other than [Trinity-2](/api-reference/trinity-2) won't support it.
